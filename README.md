# XAUUSD cBot Performance Overview

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the performance of an automated Gold (XAUUSD) trading strategy on the cTrader platform and presents the results through an interactive Excel dashboard. The dashboard converts raw trading activity into meaningful KPIs that help evaluate profitability, risk, consistency and overall strategy effectiveness.

## Terms and Definitions

- **XAUUSD:** Gold traded against the US Dollar.
- **Buy Trade:** A trade that profits when Gold prices rise.
- **Sell Trade:** A trade that profits when Gold prices fall.
- **Setup:** A complete trading opportunity generated by the strategy.
- **Position:** An individual trade executed within a setup.
- **Entry Price:** The market price at which a trade is opened.
- **Closing Price:** The market price at which a trade is closed.
- **Partial Close:** Closing part of a trade to secure profits while keeping the rest open.
- **Runner:** The final position left open to capture larger market moves.
- **Lot Size:** The volume or trade size taken in the market.
- **Net Profit:** Final profit after costs and losses.
- **Gross Profit:** Total profits from winning trades.
- **Gross Loss:** Total losses from losing trades.
- **Win Rate:** Percentage of setups that end in profit.
- **Profit Factor:** Gross Profit divided by Gross Loss.
- **Drawdown:** A decline from a previous account peak.
- **Maximum Drawdown:** The largest decline during the analysis period.
- **Balance:** Account value after completed trades.
- **Peak Balance:** Highest account value achieved.
- **Return on Account:** Percentage growth relative to starting balance.
- **Commission:** Broker execution fee.
- **Swap:** Overnight holding charge or credit.

## Objectives

- Monitor trading performance
- Evaluate profitability
- Measure trading risk
- Analyze winning and losing setups
- Track account growth over time
- Compare buy and sell performance
- Present results through interactive visuals
